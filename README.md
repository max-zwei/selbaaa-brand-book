Welcome. This is your starting point for people creating products, designs, and words for selba. You’ll find the brand foundation, guidance for your next task, and the assets to work with.

selba makes room for children to try, choose, and take part in everyday family life with help when they need it. That idea guides what we make and how we talk about it.

## Start here

New to selba? Begin with [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) for the brand’s purpose, principles, and boundaries. Then meet the families we design for in the [audience overview](personas/README.md).

Already working on something? Choose the guide that fits:

| Your task | Where to go | What you’ll find |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write or edit for selba | [Tone and voice](guidelines/tone-and-voice/tone-and-voice.md) | Writing principles, situational guidance, and German examples |
| Design for the website | [Website design system](guidelines/DESIGN.md/website.md) | Typography, layout, components, and current Figma references |
| Plan or select photographs | [Imagery](guidelines/imagery.md) | Photography direction, scene guidance, and review criteria |
| Add illustrations to photographs | [Illustrations](guidelines/illustrations.md) | Drawing style, palette rules, and how overlays relate to the image |
| Work on Schlafsteine | [Product concept](guidelines/DESIGN.md/products/Schlafsteine/product.md) | Fixed block forms, family-assigned routine stickers, intended use, and communication boundaries |
| Understand the audience in more detail | [Parents](personas/parents/parents.md) · [Children](personas/children/children.md) · [Research](personas/research.md) | Fictional profiles alongside research sources and their limits |

## Working with the book

Keep the detailed rules in their source documents rather than copying them into new guides. Use **BRAND.md** for brand intent, the specialist guides for execution, and the relevant product document for product facts.

Some designs and decisions are still being developed.

If two sources disagree, flag the difference and report to Max.
