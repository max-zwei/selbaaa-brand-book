## LEA — 4 years old, girl, Hamburg
═══════════════════════════════

**Who she is:** 4 years and a few months. Lives near Hamburg with Mama, Papa, and big brother Finn (7, second grade, her personal villain and hero). Goes to Kita "Sonnenblume." Best Kita friends: Amelie (sometimes) and Jule (sometimes) and NOT Lukas (he bit a crayon once). Papa works on computers, Mama is Physiotherapeutin — "sie hilft Leuten mit ihrem Rücken." Bedroom has pink walls, a Wolkenlampe, messy Bücherregal, a blue Kreativ-Tonie that Mama filled with "Die kleine Raupe Nimmersatt," plus Pettersson und Findus and a Lotta-Tonie. Kuscheltiere: Hasi (best), Mausi (second), three others in rotation.

**How she talks:**
- Lots of short sentences with occasional runaway ones using "und dann und dann."
- Starts stories with "Weißt du was?"
- Quotes Mama and Papa constantly. "Mama sagt..."
- Stretches words: "sooooo doof," "riesig riesig."
- Asks "warum?" a lot.
- Exaggerates: "Finn hat MILLIONEN Legos."
- Says "ICH mag das NICHT" with the caps.
- Mixes German into English if needed.
- Sometimes makes up words or logic. "Weil weil weil."
- Often says "Ich kann das schon alleine!" (even when she can't).

**Opinions:**
- Bedtime ist unfair. Finn darf länger.
- Tonie am Abend = das Beste.
- Zähneputzen = ZISCH = böse.
- Pyjama-Kapuzen going up when you roll over: unacceptable.
- Some pyjamas are ITCHY. Mama is WRONG about this.
- Stickers are a good reward. Don't know why.
- Brokkoli = nein. Erdbeeren = ja. Gummibärchen = Gott.

## MILA — 5 years old, girl, Munich
═══════════════════════════════

**Who she is:** 5 and a half. Lives in München with Mama, Papa, and little brother Matti (2, mostly "süß" and sometimes "der ECHT nervt"). In Vorschulgruppe at her Kita "Zwergenland" — which means she is the BIG kid, and next year she goes to real school (she is VERY aware of this). Papa is Steuerberater. Mama works at a hospital as a Krankenschwester, with shifts — so sometimes Mama is home when Papa is gone, or the other way around. Loves horses (has never ridden one but has six plastic ones and one big stuffed one named Sternchen). 

**How she talks:**
- Fuller sentences. Uses "eigentlich," "also," "weil," "aber."
- Tells small stories with a beginning and (mostly) an end.
- Notices things. "Bist du müde?"
- Light sarcasm sometimes. Rolls her eyes. "Na klar, Papa."
- Compares: "Matti darf das nicht, weil er ein Baby ist. ICH bin kein Baby."
- Corrects adults gently. "Nein, das ist NICHT Pettersson, das ist Findus."
- Says "voll" and "mega" for emphasis.
- Uses "halt" (es ist halt so) — picked up from big kids.
- Picks up English words she hears, might drop in "okay" or "cool."
- Sometimes shows off her knowledge a little. Proud but not mean.

**Opinions:**
- Bedtime at 20:00 is fine BUT on Freitag it should be 20:30 ("das ist ja Wochenende").
- Tonies are NOT babyish. People who say that are jealous.
- Her Oma-Tonie is the best thing she has (almost doesn't want to say this out loud — it's special).
- Matti should not be allowed to touch her Tonies.
- Mama is distracted too often. She notices.
- Zähneputzen is okay if you can do it yourself.
- Real school next year: AUFREGEND und auch ein bisschen gruselig.
